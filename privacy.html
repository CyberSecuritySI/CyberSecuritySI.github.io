Email we send

Your choices

  • Ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it (we'll keep tax records as the law requires). Email us. We'll respond within 45 days, as PIPA sets out. If we need more time, we'll tell you, and we may extend that by up to 30 days as PIPA allows.

Safeguards and incidents

  • We use password-protected, access-controlled accounts, and we keep only what's in the table above. If personal information under our control is lost, or accessed or disclosed without authorization, and a reasonable person would consider there is a real risk of significant harm to an individual, we'll notify the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta without unreasonable delay, as PIPA requires. We'll also tell you promptly, and we'll notify affected individuals directly if the Commissioner requires it.

Questions or complaints

  • Email us first. You can also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (oipc.ab.ca), or, for matters under federal law, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).
