Clear Rules support policy

Version 2026-10-17

Channel: email only, to accelerate@mail.grokbot.com. A phone or video call is available if you ask. We never ask for passwords, remote access, or payment anywhere except by e-Transfer to that address.

Hours: Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Mountain Time, excluding Alberta statutory holidays.

Our goals (targets we aim for, not guarantees): | Request | First reply goal | |---|---| | General question / plan question thread | within 2 business days | | Complaint | acknowledged within 1 business day; we aim to resolve within 5 business days | | You sent personal information by mistake | deleted, and you're told, within 1 business day | | Suspected privacy incident involving information we hold | we tell you promptly (see the Privacy Notice) |

What counts as a question thread: one topic, back and forth until it's answered. Allowances: Starter Kit has 3 threads in the first 30 days. Plans include a set number of threads a month, shown in your plan. Unused threads don't roll over. A fix to something we got wrong never counts against your allowance.

What support covers: your policy and documents, how to roll them out, whether a new tool fits your rules, and where to check a setting yourself. What it doesn't cover: legal advice; logging in to, configuring, or fixing your systems; filling in insurer or client forms; incident response or forensics. For those, we'll suggest you contact your lawyer, IT provider, or insurer.

In an emergency (money sent to a scammer, an account taken over): call your bank and your IT provider first. Report fraud to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca). Then email us, and we'll help you update your rules.

Complaints: email with "Complaint" in the subject. Outcomes follow our terms: a fix, or a refund where the terms allow. You can also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (oipc.ab.ca) about privacy matters.

Clear Rules: FAQ and knowledge base

1. Is this legal advice? No. Our documents are general guidance to help you write your own office rules. They aren't legal advice, and we don't promise compliance with any law or any insurance result. Ask your lawyer about legal questions.

2. Does my office actually need an AI policy? No law requires most small Alberta offices to have one by name. But Alberta's PIPA expects organizations to protect personal information with reasonable security arrangements. If staff paste client information into AI tools with no rules, that's hard to defend. A written policy, sign-offs, and a dated record show what you decided and when.

3. What's the difference between the free AI Check and the Starter Kit? The AI Check is 18 yes/no questions you do yourself. It shows your gaps. The Kit is the finished set of documents written for your tools and roles: policy, quick rules, sign-off form, tools register, concern form, vendor checklist, quarterly review, training walkthrough with quiz, settings cards, rollout emails, and your first dated record.

4. How long does it take? We aim to deliver your draft within 10 business days of your completed intake. You get one round of changes, then approve it.

5. Do you log in to our systems or install anything? Never. Our settings cards show you where to check, and you or your IT provider make any changes.

6. What information do you need from us? Roles ("bookkeeper"), not names; your tool list; and the types of information you handle. Never send client files, ID numbers, banking details, or passwords.

7. How do we pay? By Interac e-Transfer, up front, to accelerate@mail.grokbot.com (Autodeposit, no security question), with your invoice number in the message. Work starts once the payment shows in our bank account. We never ask for cheques, gift cards, crypto, or for you to "send back the difference".

8. Do you charge GST? Not at the moment. We aren't GST-registered. If that changes, we'll give you 30 days' notice.

9. What if we don't like the draft? You get one round of changes. If you still won't approve it, ask within 14 days of receiving it for a full refund.

10. What do the monthly plans do? They keep your rules current as AI tools change: a monthly "what we checked" note, a quarterly refresh with a new dated version, your sign-off record kept, a new-hire pack, and email questions. Plans start from CA$49/month and require a Starter Kit first.

11. Can we cancel? Any time, by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for, and you keep all your documents.

12. Do you use AI to write the documents? Yes, AI tools help with drafting. Branden reviews everything before it reaches you, and your client data never goes into those tools. We work from your roles and tool list only.

13. Will this get us a lower insurance premium or pass an insurer questionnaire? We can't promise that. We never fill in or sign insurer forms. Having written rules and a dated sign-off record can help you answer honestly.

14. Do you work with clinics or dental offices? No. Health information custodians follow Alberta's Health Information Act, which is outside what we do.

15. What happens to our information when we leave? Intake answers are deleted 30 days after delivery, question threads 12 months after they close, and a minimal service log 24 months after you leave. Invoices are kept 6 years for tax. Details are in our Privacy Notice.
