Free Scam & Leak Check

Paste an email or text you're unsure about, or something you're about to paste into ChatGPT or another AI tool. You'll get GO, CAUTION or STOP with a short "what to do".

Your text never leaves your browser. The check runs on your device only. Nothing is uploaded, saved or sent, and this page has no analytics, cookies or trackers. It makes no network requests after it loads, so you can switch Wi-Fi off and it still works.

Please read: what this is and isn't

This is a rule-based helper, not a guarantee. It looks for common warning signs, so it can miss new or unusual scams and can sometimes flag a normal message. GO only means nothing on our list was flagged. It doesn't mean the message is safe. When in doubt, call the sender on a number you already have. Don't use the number or link in the message. Never share a code you were texted, and never change payment details because of an email alone.

In our latest internal test it gave the expected answer on 37 of 39 made-up test messages (not real customer messages). It still missed one scam message in that test. Real messages can be different.

What it looks for: payment or bank-detail change requests, urgent wires, gift-card asks, "send me the code you just got" account-takeover tricks, secret "boss" favours, and in AI pastes: SINs, card and Canadian bank numbers, health card numbers, birthdates, bulk contact lists and "confidential" documents.
