Trust: how Clear Rules handles your information

Last reviewed 2026-10-17 · Owner: Branden Troutman · Next review: 2027-01-17

In one paragraph: Clear Rules is a one-person Alberta business run by Branden Troutman. We work by email only. We never log in to your systems, install anything, or monitor your staff. We ask for roles, not names, and we keep only what we need, on a fixed deletion schedule.

Where your information lives

What Where Notes
Email with us Our email provider (mail.grokbot.com) May be stored outside Canada
This website GitHub Pages No cookies, trackers, analytics or forms
Documents we draft Our working files, then emailed to you Built from your roles and tool list only

Who else handles it (subprocessors)

  • Our email provider: sending and receiving email.
  • AI drafting tools: used on your roles and tool list only, never on personal information about your clients or staff.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

Some of these may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. See our privacy notice.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Intake answers: deleted 30 days after delivery
  • Email question threads: deleted 12 months after the thread closes
  • Minimal service log: 24 months after you leave
  • Invoices: 6 years (tax law)

Testing and certification status

We hold no security certification (such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 or CyberSecure Canada), and we haven't had an external security assessment. We'll update this page with dates if that changes. Every document is reviewed by a person before it's sent to you.

How we take payment

Only by Interac e-Transfer to accelerate@mail.grokbot.com. We will never ask you to pay by cheque, gift card or crypto, to pay a different address, or to "send back the difference". If you get a message like that claiming to be us, don't pay. Email us.

Report a concern

Email accelerate@mail.grokbot.com with "Security" or "Privacy" in the subject. We aim to acknowledge within 1 business day. You can also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (oipc.ab.ca).

Available on request

  • Answers to your vendor or security questions, in writing
  • Our terms, privacy notice and sample documents
