Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Our materials are general guidance to help you write down your own office rules. They aren't legal advice, and we don't promise compliance with any law or any insurance result. For legal questions, talk to your lawyer.

Do you install anything or log in to our systems?

No. We never log in, install, configure or monitor anything. Our cards show you where to check your own settings, and you, or your IT provider, make any changes.

What data do you see?

None for the AI Check. You fill it in yourself and keep it. If you'd like your top 3 fixes, send only the question numbers. If you buy the Kit, the intake asks about roles ("front desk", "bookkeeper"), not names. Never send us client files, ID numbers, banking details or passwords.

Do we need a call or a meeting?

No. Everything works by email. If you'd prefer a quick call, just ask.

How do we pay?

By Interac e-Transfer, up front, to accelerate@mail.grokbot.com (Autodeposit, so no security question is needed). Your invoice number goes in the message. Work starts once the payment shows in our bank account. That is the only address we take payment at. We'll never ask you to pay by cheque, gift card or crypto, or to "send back the difference".

Why not just use a free template?

You can, and our free AI Check will tell you if a template is enough for you. The Kit is for offices that want the rules written around their own tools and roles, plus the staff summary, sign-off sheet and dated record ready to use, and someone to keep them current as the tools change.

Do you use AI to write the materials?

Yes, AI tools help with drafting. A person reviews everything before it reaches you, and your client data never goes into them.
